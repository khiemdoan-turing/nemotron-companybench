What this means for Nemotron

The gap is in how it works a job, not in how long it gets: four habits to train.

Find the record firstLook for the record the process itself writes before counting anything.
Read the ruleOpen the policy page the job points to, and try another way in when the body comes back empty.
Get connected fastUse the standard client and handle session errors, so the steps go to the job.
Retry, then say soRetry a failed email or ticket in another format, and report a blocker instead of making a figure up.
Appendix

How the test works

The jobs40 real company jobs on Turing CompanyBench. Each model runs every job 3 times: 120 runs per model.
The agentTerminus 2, with internet access.
Time limit80 minutes per run by default. Runs that hit the limit were run again with up to 5 hours.
Reasoning effortSuper ran at medium, the highest Data OS offers for it. Ultra and the frontier models ran at high.
ModelsNemotron 3.5 Super (120B), Nemotron 3 Ultra (550B), Claude Fable 5.1, GPT 6.1 Sol, GPT 6 Astra, Gemini 4 Argon.
Step-by-step readEvery Super, Ultra and Claude run on the 8 jobs where Nemotron trails most (24 runs per model), counted against five failure modes. A job counts when a mode shows in at least one of its 3 runs. Quotes are Nemotron’s own notes.
CaveatsTwo Super reruns had not finished when the data was pulled. One Ultra 5-hour rerun was graded with a newer scorer than its 80 minute run.
